Google Meet — Meeting Room Intelligence

Senior Software Engineer & TL / 2016 – 2021 / New York

I led the AI features on Google Meet's room hardware. The main thing I shipped was AutoZoom, a model that tracks whoever's in the room and frames the camera around them, running entirely on the device.

I also built the inference infrastructure underneath it: the pipelines that keep a real-time frame budget on modest, fixed hardware in environments you can’t control.
